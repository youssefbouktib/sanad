# Atlas Institute of Technology — Student Handbook (fictional, 2026–2027)

This handbook is a fictional document created to demonstrate Sanad. It describes no real institution.

## Attendance

Attendance at lab sessions is mandatory. A student who misses more than two lab sessions in a semester must meet the program director before being allowed to sit the final exam.

Lectures are recorded and published on the learning platform within 24 hours, but recordings do not replace lab attendance.

## Project deadlines

Capstone project submissions close on 15 November 2026 at 23:59 (Casablanca time). Submissions are made through the learning platform only; projects sent by email are not accepted.

## Extensions

Extension requests must be sent to the course coordinator at least 48 hours before the deadline. A medical certificate is required for extensions longer than 3 days. The maximum extension is 7 days.

## Grading

The final grade is computed as 40% capstone project, 35% final exam and 25% continuous assessment. A student must obtain at least 10/20 in the final exam to validate the course, regardless of the overall average.

## Academic integrity

Using AI assistants is allowed for brainstorming and debugging, but every use must be declared in the project report. Submitting AI-generated code without declaring it is treated as plagiarism.
